# Applicant and student guide

## Applying
1. **Register** at `/registration` with your own email and CNIC. Passwords need 10+ characters with upper and lower case letters and a number.
2. **Sign in** at `/login` and open **Apply Online**.
3. Fill in your matric and intermediate results and rank up to three programs.
4. Upload documents: JPG, PNG or WebP up to 5 MB, or PDF up to 10 MB. Every file is virus-scanned; PDFs with scripts or attachments are refused.
5. Track the decision on your dashboard. After approval, download the fee challan, pay at the bank, and upload the stamped copy.

## After admission
- Sign in at `/student-login` with the student ID (for example `26BSSE007`) and the temporary password from the admissions office. You will be asked to choose your own password first.
- The dashboard shows subjects, timetable, attendance (75% is needed for exams), marks, announcements, fee challans and exam slips.
- Use **Report an issue** for corrections, and **attendance appeal** if you were absent for a documented reason. One appeal per semester can be open at a time.
