# Teacher and staff guide

## Teachers (`/teachers/login.php`)
- First sign-in asks you to replace the temporary password.
- **Attendance**: choose a subject and date (today or up to a year back), mark each student, save.
- **Marks**: choose subject and assessment, enter marks against the total.
- **Announcements**: post to one subject or to all your students.
- You only see subjects an admin has assigned to you.

## Staff (`/staff/login.php`)
- Heads of Department see the department's teachers and subjects.
- Other roles see their own profile and dashboard.
